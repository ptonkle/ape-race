## Calls I made (the integrator)

   - less drag in space;
   - on odd-angle vines, W keeps driving the swing up to about twice the usual speed;
   - a "slingshot" boost when you let go of an odd vine pointing the right way.
   - Touching a debris piece adds more. Hard cap about 85 m/s (306 km/h).
   - **Question:** does that feel right, or should the odd vines' own gravity pull the ape too? That would give real slingshots, but it would go against "sets the vines' direction only".
2. **The reset.** Crossing the boundary puts you at once on the nearest deck that isn't ahead of you, standing still. **Question:** should you keep some speed, or wait out a short delay?
3. **The door.** It shuts when anyone swings the switch vine more than about 55° forward. It stays shut 5 s and can't be re-shut until it has reopened. **Question:** is 5 s right?
4. **The Great Fox pass** is timed to the race clock (one pass for everyone), not to each player's own position.
