## The person's answers (2026-10-16)

- **Scale:** "tower 1 is 2x as tall as it originally was since you essentially climb it in two different segments, tower 2 is 60% the height of tower 1. bridges take about 10 seconds to cross."
- **The drop:** "It's a vertical tube. Look at the Minecraft Dropper adventure map as inspiration." (A walled tube you fall down, steering round obstacles; touching one costs you.)
- **The drop's length:** "300m but way more pads to dodge than that, like 20 to 30."
- **The vines at the bottom:** "it's anchored to the top of the horizontal exit tunnel. it's a set of vines in a line like at the beginning, except they hang near horizontally."
- Numbers are to be given **as comparisons to the first design**, not in metres.

## The drawing, as read

- Tower 1 (left, three tiers, the tall one) and tower 2 (right, one tier, shorter). Two straight bridges join them: a lower one near the ground and an upper one at tower 2's top.

The blue line: along under the lower bridge to tower 2, up tower 2's outside (the green ticks on its wall are vines), back along under the upper bridge (green ticks under both bridges are vines hanging from their undersides), in through the brown doorway in tower 1, up inside tower 1, out of its top and sideways through the purple portal.

## Requirements, as a checklist

- [ ] Two big towers joined by two long straight bridges, no labels. Tower 1 twice the first design's keep height; tower 2 60% of tower 1; each bridge about 10 s of swinging.
- [ ] Start at the foot of tower 1.
- [ ] Leg 1: swing under the lower bridge to tower 2, on vines hung from its underside.
- [ ] Leg 2: climb the outside of tower 2.
- [ ] Leg 3: swing under the upper bridge back to tower 1.
- [ ] Leg 4: through a doorway; climb tower 1's inside on scattered vines with no obvious helix; the player finds their own way up. Tower 1 wide enough to swing up inside with momentum.
- [ ] Leg 5: out of the top of tower 1 through the portal (the wizard's tear).
- [ ] The portal world: a vertical tube, Dropper-style, about 300 m, with 20–30 bounce pads to dodge by air control; no vines; the aim is to keep falling as fast as possible.
- [ ] At the bottom, a line of vines hung from the top of the horizontal exit tunnel's mouth, about 10° below horizontal; grabbed at speed they sling you along the tunnel to the finish line, which you reach without landing.
- [ ] The portal world keeps its look (the void colours, the creatures, the zip) in the new shape.
- [ ] The portal (the tear) is not flat: it moves, taking after Sector Ape's black hole (its turning bands, jets and drawn-in sparkles), without being as complex.
- [ ] The trampoline room is gone. (The sub-tower branches and the mirrored zipper go with the old course.)
- [ ] Kept from the first design: the palette, skies and weathers, the wizard and witch costumes, the flame cat, mages and dragons as casters, climb vines' lift, forgiving resets with a boost, solid stone, item crates in rows.
- [ ] The other maps play exactly the same.
- [ ] Lobby spot, flyover, map card, medals, AI ordering, step and frame cost, colourblind and JOJO MODE, patch notes, README, TRIAGE, SECOND-ORDER, journal `#tower`.
